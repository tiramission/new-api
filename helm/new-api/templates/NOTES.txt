=======================================================================
  IMPORTANT — production checklist
=======================================================================
- Replace all default passwords in your external database/Redis:
    postgresql.auth.password / mysql.auth.password / redis.auth.password
- For multi-replica deployments (new-api.replicaCount > 1) you MUST set:
    extraSecretEnv.SESSION_SECRET = "<random-string>"
  and ensure a shared database + Redis is used.
- Configure Ingress + TLS for HTTPS in front of the gateway.
- Review resource requests/limits and PVC sizes for your workload.
- (Optional) Point LOG_SQL_DSN at a separate database or ClickHouse for logs
  by setting externalDatabase.logDsn.
